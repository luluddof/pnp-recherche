## 15:00 structural remarks [P]
- Translation symmetry of the Pauli model: P_{Tv} M P_v^{-1} ~ M for all v (M is a Clifford), and X -> P_{Tv} X P_v^{-1} maps
  Pauli supports by s -> s + (T+1)v = s + T^2 v (bijective since T^2 = T+1 invertible): supports matter up to translation,
  register projections are translated (|pi_i| invariant). Analogue of Bell's twisted translations (prob. representations).
- Full 3-register Pauli group = G x G^perp (G^perp = {R(x)R(x)R}, multiplicity algebra M_N, each element ring cost 1).
  sigma = M (x) 1_N. Using multiplicity Paulis does not reduce to the Pauli model (twirling loses a factor N^2): the general
  model has strictly more room than the Pauli model; no reduction either way known.
- Products of edge operators: sigma = S12 S23 = S23 S31 = S31 S12 per site; any site-split into rotated composites gives
  sigma = [layer of 3 edge-SWAPs on k/3 sites] x [another], ring 2^{2k eta} (same), error 6 * 2^{-k delta/3} >> 2 * 2^{-k delta}:
  fewer, larger pieces win (convexity of delta(eta)); 3-factor forms sigma = O12 O23 O31 with O_ij in span{1, S_ij} are
  impossible beyond the 2-edge ones and cost like SWAP each. => the composite is the optimum of the "edge-product" class.
